Catch URBAN BANDITS at the 1st LOUD Fest Manila! ⚡️

THE COMMENT SECTION MEDIA NETWORK•Oct 28, 2026 · 8:00 AM PHT

Pioneering Pinoy punk band Urban Bandits is composed of vocalist Arnold Morales, guitarist Ferdie dela Cruz, bassist Vinty Lava, and drummer Rogel dela Cruz.

Formed in 1984, Urban Bandits emerged from Manila’s underground music scene as one of the pioneering forces of Filipino punk rock. Their debut cassette album, Independence Day (1985), released through the independent underground network, captured the frustration, defiance, and restless spirit of a generation. Tracks like “No Future sa Pader,” “Manila Girl,” and “Breaking the Wall” became part of their enduring legacy.

Though the band disbanded in 1987, their music continued to inspire generations of Filipino punk rockers. Returning to the stage in 2017, Urban Bandits proved that punk is more than a passing trend—it's an attitude, a statement, and a refusal to conform. From the underground tape-trading days to reunion gigs and anniversary shows, these veterans remain part of the foundation of Pinoy punk.
